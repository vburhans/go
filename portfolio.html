PoE Emergency System - Soteria

As part of a project as a student of Information Technology and Management at Illinois Tech

Both as a volunteer and as a part of coursework; I made hardware, software, and promotional improvements to the Soteria PoE Emergency System. It wields PoE technology's flexibility to improve the speed and clarity of emergency alerts in a building. A demo model is built with CO and smoke sensors, a raspberry pi, and a small display for detailed air quality status.
